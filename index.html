⚖️ Official Verification & Advisory Notice

All grievances lodged through this portal are treated as formal alerts and are subject to verification by qualified FDA enforcement officers. Citizens are requested to provide accurate shop/vendor locations, product batch details, and genuine photographic evidence to assist swift inspection.

⚠️ Important Notice Regarding AI Prioritization:
AI-assisted priority is only a preliminary recommendation and does not replace official investigation. Automated scores assist officers in triaging urgent public health risks and never approve, reject, or close complaints autonomously.
Student / Academic Project Notice:
This web portal is developed strictly as a demonstration project. It is not affiliated with the Maharashtra State Government or the Food and Drug Administration. For emergency health crises, please contact your nearest municipal health authority or call official emergency services.
